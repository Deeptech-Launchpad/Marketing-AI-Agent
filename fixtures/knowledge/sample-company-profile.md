# Meridian Catalog Services — Company Profile (SYNTHETIC)

## Who we are

Meridian Catalog Services is a fictional product-data company used as test
material. In this invented scenario, Meridian was founded in 2016 and employs
around 90 people across two invented offices. It exists only so that the
knowledge pipeline has a coherent `company_info` document to chunk, embed and
retrieve.

## What we do, in one line

Meridian takes the incomplete product catalogs that industrial and B2B
distributors have accumulated, and turns them into structured, complete,
searchable product records.

## Why the company exists

The invented origin story: the founders previously ran fulfilment operations for
a distributor and spent most of their week answering phone calls asking for
dimensions, materials and compliance certificates that were printed on the
manufacturer's own datasheet but had never been entered into the webshop. The
data existed. It was simply not where the buyer was looking.

## Market position

Meridian positions itself as a specialist rather than a generalist agency. It
does not do brand design, paid media buying, or general web development. It does
one thing: product data. That narrowness is deliberate and is the main thing
that distinguishes it from full-service digital agencies in the same space.

## How the company describes its own difference

- Work is done against the customer's live platform, not in a spreadsheet that
  someone later has to import by hand.
- Enrichment is verified against manufacturer source documents, not generated
  from the existing product title.
- Every delivered attribute carries a provenance record showing which source
  document it came from.

## Operating model

Engagements are scoped by catalog size and attribute depth rather than by hours.
A typical invented engagement covers between 2,000 and 40,000 SKUs. Work is
delivered in batches so the customer sees enriched records on their storefront
within the first few weeks rather than at the end.

## Geographies served (invented)

North America and the United Kingdom, with a small number of engagements in
Australia. Meridian does not currently operate in the EU in this scenario.

## What Meridian is not

Not a PIM vendor. Not a reseller of product data feeds. Not a marketplace
listing service. Meridian does not sell software licences; it sells the
completed data and the process that produced it.
