## Notes on what has worked (invented)

Openers that name a specific observed gap outperform openers that describe
Meridian. The "send us five SKUs" offer is answered more often than "book a
call", because it asks for less commitment and produces something the recipient
can look at. Posts that describe the buyer's own workflow outperform posts that
describe the service.
